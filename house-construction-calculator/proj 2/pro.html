Homeowner awareness

Know what to ask before you build.

A useful estimate is not just a number. Understand what is included, what is excluded and what needs professional checking.

Ask for an itemised quotation.

Request clear quantities, material specifications, labour, finishing scope, payment milestones and exclusions in writing.

Plan for more than the structure.

Check whether approvals, architect fees, soil testing, utility connections, compound walls, interiors and taxes are included.
